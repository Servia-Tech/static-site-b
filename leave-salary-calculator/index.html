Note that the 30 days are calendar days, not working days — so weekends falling inside a leave period are counted. This is one of the most common misunderstandings about UAE annual leave, and it is why 30 days of entitlement is closer to 22 working days in practice.

The basic-versus-gross trap

This is where most employees are short-changed, and the rule is not intuitive:

  • When you take annual leave during employment, you are paid your normal full salary including allowances.
  • When unused leave is encashed at the end of service, it is paid on basic salary only.

On a gross of AED 10,000 with a basic of AED 6,000, each encashed day is worth AED 200, not AED 333. Across 30 days that is a difference of about AED 4,000.

How the daily rate is worked out

daily leave rate = monthly basic ÷ 30

Then multiply by the number of unused days. The divisor is 30 regardless of how many days are in the actual month.

Can my employer make me forfeit unused leave?

No. Unused annual leave that you were entitled to cannot simply be cancelled at the end of service — it must be paid. What an employer can generally do during employment is set the timing of leave and require you to take it, and many contracts limit how much leave can be carried forward from one year to the next. A carry-forward limit is not the same as forfeiting leave you accrued in the year you left.

Frequently asked questions

How is leave salary calculated in the UAE?

Divide your monthly basic salary by 30 to get a daily rate, then multiply by the number of unused annual leave days. Encashment at the end of service is paid on basic salary, not gross.

How many annual leave days do I get in the UAE?

30 calendar days a year once you have completed one year of service. Between six and twelve months you accrue two days per month. Under six months there is no statutory entitlement.

Are the 30 days working days or calendar days?

Calendar days. Weekends and public holidays falling inside your leave period are counted within the 30 days, which is why the practical figure is closer to 22 working days.

Is leave encashment paid on basic or gross salary?

Basic salary when it is encashed at the end of service. When you actually take leave during employment you are paid your full normal salary including allowances. The difference catches out most employees.

Can my employer refuse to pay for unused leave?

No. Leave you have accrued and not taken must be paid on termination. Contracts may limit carry-forward between years, but that is different from cancelling leave accrued in your final year.

Do public holidays count against my annual leave?

Public holidays are a separate paid entitlement when you are working. But if a public holiday falls inside your annual leave, Article 29(7) of Federal Decree-Law 33/2021 counts it as part of the leave, unless your contract or company regulations are more favourable.
