Free Zone Gratuity in the UAE

Most free zones follow the federal formula. Two do not — and the difference is worth tens of thousands of dirhams.

Last reviewed: 26 September 2026 · General information, not legal advice

The rule of thumb

The UAE has more than forty free zones. For end-of-service purposes they fall into two groups:

GroupFree zonesEnd of service
Federal-aligned (the large majority)JAFZA, DMCC, DAFZA, Dubai South, Dubai Silicon Oasis, SAIF Zone, Hamriyah, RAKEZ, Ajman Free Zone, KIZAD, Masdar City and othersThe federal 21/30 day gratuity formula
Separate financial free zonesDIFC and ADGMDIFC: DEWS savings plan instead of gratuity. ADGM: its own gratuity rules (21/30 days' basic wage, capped at two years' wage), or an employer pension alternative

The distinction exists because DIFC and ADGM are financial free zones established under the UAE Constitution with the power to make their own civil and commercial law. Ordinary free zones are administrative, not legislative — they can set their own procedures but not displace federal employment law.

If you work in a federal-aligned free zone

Your entitlement is the standard calculation:

  • Daily wage = monthly basic salary ÷ 30
  • 21 days of basic pay per year for the first five years
  • 30 days of basic pay per year after five
  • One year minimum service; capped at two years' wages
  • Payable within 14 days of the contract ending

What genuinely differs: the process

Where free zones diverge is in administration, and this catches people out when something goes wrong:

  • Your employment contract is registered with the free zone authority, not MOHRE
  • Visa cancellation runs through the free zone's own immigration channel
  • Disputes usually go to the free zone authority first, not the MOHRE call centre
  • Some free zones require an employer to deposit a bank guarantee per employee, which can be a genuine advantage if the employer fails

How to find out which regime applies to you

Do not rely on the office address — a company in a Dubai tower can be licensed anywhere. Check instead:

  1. Your employment contract. It names the issuing authority on the first page or in the signature block.
  2. Your residence visa and labour card. These name the sponsoring authority.
  3. Your employer's trade licence. Ask HR for a copy; the licensing body is printed on it.

If the licence says DIFC, you are in the DEWS savings-scheme regime and the 21/30 day formula does not describe your entitlement. If it says ADGM, the ADGM Employment Regulations 2019 apply: a similar 21/30-day gratuity on basic wage, unless your employer offers an approved pension alternative. Anything else, and the federal formula almost certainly does.

A common misconception about free zone contracts

Some free zone employers present their internal employment regulations as though they override the federal law entirely. In general they cannot reduce statutory minimums for employees outside DIFC and ADGM. A free zone rule or contract clause that gives you less than the federal entitlement is on weak ground; one that gives you more is perfectly valid and enforceable.

The same principle applies to contract terms generally — the federal law sets a floor, not a ceiling. An employer may always be more generous.

Frequently asked questions

Do free zone employees get gratuity in the UAE?

Yes, in almost all free zones, using the same federal formula of 21 days of basic salary per year for the first five years and 30 days after. The exceptions are DIFC, which replaced gratuity with the DEWS savings plan, and ADGM, which has its own Employment Regulations with a similar 21/30-day gratuity.

Is JAFZA or DMCC gratuity different from mainland?

Not in the amount. Both follow the federal 21/30 day formula. What differs is administration — contract registration, visa cancellation and dispute handling go through the free zone authority rather than MOHRE.

Which UAE free zones do not pay traditional gratuity?

DIFC. It replaced end-of-service gratuity with the DEWS workplace savings plan in 2020. ADGM, the other financial free zone, still has a gratuity under section 59 of its Employment Regulations 2019 (21 days' basic wage a year for the first five years, 30 after, capped at two years' wage), although employers may offer a pension scheme instead.

How do I know which free zone I work in?

Check your employment contract, residence visa or your employer's trade licence — each names the licensing authority. The physical office location is not a reliable guide.

Can a free zone contract give me less gratuity than federal law?

Outside DIFC and ADGM, generally no. Federal entitlements act as a floor. A contract offering more than the statutory minimum is valid; one offering less is on weak ground.

Who do I complain to if a free zone employer will not pay?

Start with the free zone authority that registered your contract, as they handle disputes in the first instance. If that route fails, the matter can escalate to the courts.
